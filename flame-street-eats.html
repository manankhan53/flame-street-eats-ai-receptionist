How it works

One phone call, four automated steps, zero manual re-entry.

01

Call comes in

The AI answers immediately, every time — no hold music, no busy signal.

02

Order gets built

Menu questions, customizations, quantities, delivery or pickup — all handled in a natural conversation.

03

Order gets confirmed

The full order is read back before anything is finalized, so nothing gets lost.

04

Kitchen gets notified

The order lands in the queue and staff get an email the moment it's placed.
